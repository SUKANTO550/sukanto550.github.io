01 · About

Where heat, flow & sunlight meet.

I'm an M.Sc. student in Materials and Metallurgical Engineering at BUET, with a B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from KUET. I study how heat and fluid flow shape the performance of solar cells, and I'm now focused on next-generation perovskite photovoltaics.

02 · Research

What I've been simulating.

From bifacial solar modules to heat exchangers and supersonic airfoils, I solve thermal-fluid problems with CFD and validate the results.

☀️
Present · Independent

Cooling bifacial solar cells

Bifacial modules collect light on both faces, and the extra heat lowers their efficiency. I model heat transfer and cooling mechanisms to recover it, and I'm preparing numerical and experimental studies.

Corrugated plate heat exchanger

I ran CFD simulations of heat transfer and fluid flow in corrugated channels in ANSYS Fluent, validated them against theory, and presented the work at ICMIEE-2022.

03 · Interactive lab

Heat a solar panel. Then cool it.

This is the problem my research tackles. Hotter cells convert less light into electricity. Drag the sliders to see why cooling matters.

Illustrative model, not results from my study. Cell temperature follows the Faiman correlation, Tc = Ta + G / (U₀ + U₁·v + h), with U₀ = 25 W/m²K and U₁ = 6.84 W·s/m³K. Efficiency η = ηref[1 − β(Tc − 25 °C)], with ηref = 21 % and β = 0.4 %/°C, typical of crystalline silicon. Power is for a 2 m² module.
